The work

What we actually make

Some of it is used every day. Some of it is read. Some projects need both before they make sense. These are the kinds of work that keep finding their way here.

Software

Websites, applications, and internal tools. Sometimes the job is a clear front door; sometimes it's making a repetitive task easier. The useful version matters more than the impressive description.

Publishing

Writing is work here, not decoration around the software. We publish things meant to be read on their own. A page has to earn someone's attention even when there isn't a button on it.

In between

A project can need a site, a system behind it, and words that make sense of both. Those pieces should feel like one thing to the person using it, even when they were made in different ways.

About Unwritten Co.

An independent company for software, publishing, and projects that cross the boundary between them. It's easier to say what we work on than to give the whole company one tidy job title.

The company

We build our own projects. That might mean a website, a piece of software used behind the scenes, or writing with a life of its own. The common thread isn't a market category. It's having room to shape the thing from its first question to what someone finally sees.

Some work is public; some is still taking shape. This site isn't an exhaustive list or a pitch deck. It's a place to say what kind of company this is while the work itself changes.

How we work

We try things while they're small enough to change. A first version gives us something real to argue with; then we revise the parts that don't hold up. Code, design, and language each have their own work to do. One isn't a substitute for the others.

There is room here for careful maintenance as well as a new idea. Not every useful thing needs an announcement.

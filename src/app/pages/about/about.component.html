About

Unwritten Co.

A small studio that designs, builds, and runs digital products.

What we are

A small, independent studio. One founder, and a team that works through the night. We keep the company small on purpose and the standards high.

What we build

Websites, web applications, and internal tools. We are a product studio, not an agency. Most of what we make is ours, and now and then we make something for someone else.

Right now that includes Ana's Journal, an operations platform that runs the studio, and a handful of smaller tools and sites at various stages of done.

How it runs

Quietly, and mostly at night. Work is reviewed before it ships, and the systems that run the studio are held to the same bar as the things it sells.

Elsewhere

The studio's director keeps a journal. It is fiction, mostly, and it updates most nights.

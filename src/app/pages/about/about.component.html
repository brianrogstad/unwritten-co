About

Unwritten Co.

An independent product studio, founded by Brian Rogstad.

The studio

Unwritten Co. is a small studio that designs, builds, and runs digital products: websites, web applications, and the internal tools behind them.

We are a product studio, not an agency. Most of what we make is our own. Now and then we make something for someone else, and it gets the same care.

How the work is made

We build with AI, the way any studio works with good tools. It lets a small shop take on more than its size suggests. It doesn't replace judgment.

Every piece of work is reviewed before it ships. Nothing goes out just because a tool says it's done.

After launch

Launch is the middle of the story, not the end. We operate what we put into the world. We keep it running, keep it current, and fix what breaks.

If we can't keep a product running well, we don't ship it.

Correspondence for the studio goes to support@theunwrittencompany.com.

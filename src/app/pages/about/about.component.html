About

Unwritten Co.

An independent company with work in software and publishing. Those are broad labels, which is useful. The work has not arranged itself into one line of business, so we haven't forced it.

The arrangement

We develop our own projects and maintain the systems around them. Some have a public name and an audience. Others are the small tools and decisions that let the visible work keep going. A company can spend a good deal of time on those without putting them in a portfolio.

Projects here do not need to resemble one another to belong together. A publication can stand on its own; a website can be the whole thing or the front door to a larger system. The company gives both a place without making either explain the other.

What the labels cover

Software means websites and applications, and also the less conspicuous things behind them. A public screen is only one part of a working system. Sometimes the useful part is the one nobody sees.

Publishing means writing released to be read, not a stream of promotional copy for the software. The two kinds of work do meet. When they do, we let that project take its own shape rather than decide its department in advance.

After it goes live

A site doesn't stop needing work when it has an address. Links break, an archive grows, and a tool that suited its first use may not suit the next one. Revisions and maintenance are part of the project, not a separate footnote.

Writing needs to be edited, published, and findable once it is no longer the newest thing on the page. Keeping it available is work of its own.

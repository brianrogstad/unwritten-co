About

Unwritten Co.

An independent product studio. We design, build, and run our own digital products, and we keep them running long after they ship.

The name

Every product starts as something nobody has written yet. A rough idea, a problem someone keeps running into, a page that should exist and doesn't. The name comes from that stage, before the plan, when the thing could still go a dozen ways.

That stage is where most of the real work happens. The job is deciding which of those dozen ways is worth building, then writing it down properly: in design, in code, and in the words on the screen. The name is a reminder of how much is still open at the start, and how much care it takes to close it well.

What we make

Unwritten Co. is a small studio that designs, builds, and runs digital products: websites, web applications, and the internal tools that keep them going.

Most of what we make is our own. That's a choice. Owning the work means we decide when it's ready, we live with every decision inside it, and we're still here when something needs fixing a year later. When we make something for someone else, it gets the same treatment.

From idea to release

A piece of work starts with a short written brief that says what the thing is, who it's for, and what it has to do. If we can't say that plainly, it isn't ready to build.

Design and code come next, close together and by the same hands. The interface, the content, and the engineering are worked out as one product rather than passed down a line. Before anything ships, it's checked across browsers and screen sizes, against accessibility standards, and against the brief it started from.

Release is deliberate. Some things wait longer than planned because they weren't right yet. We would rather ship late than ship something we'd have to apologize for.

Tools and judgment

We build with AI, the way any studio works with good tools. It takes on a share of the drafting, checking, and routine upkeep, and it lets a small shop handle more than its size suggests.

It doesn't decide what's worth making, or when something is good enough to put in front of people. Those calls stay human. Every piece of work is reviewed before it ships, and nothing goes out because a tool reports that it's done.

After launch

Launch is the middle of the story, not the end. We operate what we put into the world. We watch it, keep it current, update what ages, and fix what breaks.

That changes how things get built in the first place. Work you plan to look after for years is built differently from work you plan to hand off. It's simpler where it can be, consistent with what already exists, and documented so the next change is an easy one. If we can't keep a product running well, we don't ship it.

Working with us

We take on outside work occasionally, when a project fits the way we work and can have our full attention. That usually means a website or web application that needs to be designed, built, and looked after by the same people from start to finish.

There is no rate card or intake form here. Inquiries come in by email, and a short description of the project and what it needs to do is the most useful place to start. When something isn't a fit, we say so plainly.
